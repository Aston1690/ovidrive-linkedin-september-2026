Cost management just jumped from third to first on the list of what keeps CFOs up at night.
Deloitte's Q1 2026 CFO Signals survey polled 200 CFOs at North American organisations with at least a billion dollars in annual revenue. Fifty two percent now name cost management their most worrying internal risk, the top response. Six months earlier it ranked third, at forty seven percent.
That is a North American finding, but the pressure behind it is not regional. Rising technology investment, thinner margins and more scrutiny on spend are not unique to one market.
The same survey found forty nine percent citing pressure to invest in new technology, cloud and AI, as a driver of that cost concern, almost level with shrinking margins at forty eight percent.
Fleet sits inside that concern more often than it gets credit for. It is one of the largest line items most of these organisations carry, split across suppliers, contracts and countries, and it rarely gets the same financial scrutiny as headcount or capital spend.
If cost management has genuinely become the top internal risk on CFOs' own list, the categories still running on spreadsheets and supplier portals are exactly where that risk is hiding.
