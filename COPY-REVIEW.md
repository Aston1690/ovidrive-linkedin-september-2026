# Copy review — before publication

Design copy and captions have been preserved from the supplied calendar. These are design-review exports; factual corrections below have not been silently applied.

1. **Post 1 — 72% cloud budget claim:** the figure appears on a vendor white-paper page, but the underlying research, sample and reporting period were not established. Confirm the original survey before publishing this headline. The FinOps Foundation also describes the 90% SaaS figure as organisations that manage SaaS **or plan to**, rather than all currently managing it.
   Sources: https://teleglobals.com/whitepaper/cloud-finops and https://data.finops.org/
2. **Post 3 — electric van growth:** identify the year and geographic scope of the 40% figure. The current IEA 2026 report gives global electric LCV growth of 45% for 2025 and China growth of 40%. Do not treat a historic or regional statistic as a current global one.
   Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/trends-in-other-ev-modes
3. **Post 4 — CFO risk:** the headline reflects Deloitte's Q1 2026 North American survey. For a September post, “just became” needs reconsideration: the newer Q2 dashboard says cost management fell to fifth. Date and scope should be clear.
   Sources: https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/topics/business-strategy-growth/1q-2026-cfo-signals-survey.html and https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/topics/leadership/cfo-survey-data-dashboard.html
4. **Post 5 — agent adoption:** Gartner's baseline is **less than 5%**, rather than exactly 5%. The 40% figure is a forecast for the end of 2026. The claim that adoption is faster than cloud, mobile-first and generative AI is not established by that press release.
   Source: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-08-26-gartner-predicts-40-percent-of-enterprise-apps-will-feature-task-specific-ai-agents-by-2026-up-from-less-than-5-percent-in-2025
5. **Post 6 — cancellations:** Gartner says **over 40%**, as a prediction for the end of 2027. The two 40% statistics describe different populations. The source copy's “Same number” construction would need reconsideration if the qualifier is restored.
   Source: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027

The Hackett Group source supports the procurement workload increase and declining resources:
https://www.thehackettgroup.com/the-hackett-group-reports-rapid-progress-in-procurements-ai-agenda/

Fortune Business Insights supports the main fleet-software market figures and its technology FAQ gives the 23.4% AI/analytics rate:
